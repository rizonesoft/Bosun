## Part 2. Excellence

**Microsoft PowerToys Awake** is the strongest alternative for the keep-awake portion. It can attach to a specified process or its parent, supports timed operation, and releases its Windows power request on exit without editing the power plan. Bosun additionally owns screen-saver lock restoration. The candidate does not yet beat Awake on crash recovery or preservation of power settings. [Microsoft PowerToys Awake documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/powertoys/awake)

1. `suggestion` **Reduce persistent machine changes.** Target zero persistent AC/DC power-plan edits for a supported campaign, and verify that a power preference the owner changes during the run survives its end.
2. `suggestion` **Release the power request with the campaign process.** Target normal Windows power behavior within one second of forced launcher termination, with zero subsequent hooks, status reads or scheduled tasks. Measure this separately from recovery of the screen-saver lock.
3. `suggestion` **Support shorter timed holds.** Awake accepts durations in seconds. Bosun’s shortest hold is one hour. Target a ten-minute campaign hold that expires at the requested time while the session is live.
