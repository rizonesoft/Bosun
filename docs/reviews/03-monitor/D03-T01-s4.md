**Part 2: excellence**

Competitor: **HWiNFO**, the strongest directly comparable configurable local sensor-alert alternative considered here. Its author documents configurable polling and threshold sample counts, including a one-second polling example, plus minimum spacing between alarms. This is documented capability, not a benchmark I ran. [HWiNFO alert configuration](https://www.hwinfo.com/forum/threads/alerting-settings.5565/), [polling configuration](https://www.hwinfo.com/forum/threads/supported-command-line-parameters-in-hwinfo64-pro.8549/).

1. **suggestion**: Alert latency is materially weaker. Bosun combines a 30-second logger interval with an independent 30-second check. A sustained crossing can therefore wait nearly 60 seconds, plus execution and scheduling time; a shorter dip can be missed entirely. HWiNFO can evaluate much more frequently. **Target:** measure sustained-crossing-to-notification latency and achieve a maximum of two seconds under the representative build workload, retaining one notice per episode and the existing idle budget. Improve sampling in the owning logger/tool.

2. **suggestion**: The threshold’s practical meaning is hidden behind a tooltip, and the notification predicts a freeze from available RAM alone. `PressureCheck.cs:49` says “before Windows freezes”, although this measurement does not establish that outcome. **Target:** visible helper text identifying Windows available memory, the check cadence and recovery margin; notification wording that describes the measured shortage without promising a freeze. Verify normal/error golden images at minimum window size and 200% scaling.

3. **suggestion**: Notification navigation does not take the person directly to memory investigation. `NativeTrayIcon.cs:129` opens the panel, and `TrayController.cs:99` restores its current page. From Settings, responding requires the notification click plus selecting Memory. **Target:** one click from the pressure notification to Memory, preserving the unsaved-edit question. This is a proposed advantage, not a measured HWiNFO click-count claim.

I found no supported competitive disadvantage to report for default setup steps or saved-setting recovery. Screen-reader operation, scaling and runtime resource use were not exercised, so superiority in those areas remains unproven.
